Five Concepts about HTML

1. Tags

Tags tell the browser how to organize content.

2. Headings

Headings are used for titles and subtitles.

3. Paragraphs

Paragraphs display text on a webpage.

4. Links

Links take users to other pages or websites.

5. Images

Images add pictures to a webpage.
